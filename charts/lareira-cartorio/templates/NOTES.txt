    -o jsonpath='{.status.phase}{"\n"}'  # expect "Compliant"

  # 3. Resource inventory:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get all,signaturegate,compliancepolicy,prometheusrule \
    -l pleme.io/part-of=openclaw

What this service is:

  cartorio is the central merkle ledger for the openclaw stack. It
  admits OCI images, helm charts, and skills only with valid tameshi
  attestation chains. Every other compliance gate (Zot pre-push hook,
  Kyverno verifyImages, openclaw-scanner re-attestation) reads from it.

Next steps:

  • Wire the Zot push pre-hook to call /api/v1/artifacts/by-digest/{digest}
    (rejects PUTs whose digest isn't admitted)
  • Configure Kyverno verifyImages to call /api/v1/artifacts/{id}/proof
    and verify the inclusion proof against the pinned ledger root
  • Point openclaw-scanner at /api/v1/artifacts/{id}/verify for re-attestation
